## Plan: "Think First, Discretize Later" — Website Explainer Page

A new research explainer page on `Adrian-Mag.github.io` that serves as **supplementary
material for the EGU 2026 poster** (`media/research/posters/poster_EGU_26.pdf`). It walks
a calculus-plus-linear-algebra audience from a naive boxcar inversion that "looks fine" to
the reveal that the model space, its norm/geometry, and the adjoint were silently chosen —
with a signature UX of clickable "?" popups for the deeper concepts.

Figures and data are generated by **Python scripts that live in this repo** (under
`figure_generation/think_first_discretize_later/`) and import **`intervalinf`/`pygeoinf`**
from the `inferences` conda env. They write static images + JSON into
`media/research/think-first/`. The shipped website itself stays plain HTML5/CSS3 + vanilla
JS (no build step), per `AGENTS.md`; the figure scripts are **offline tooling, not served
content**.

**Theory backbone:** `discretization.tex` (three strategies Discretize/Update/Push and the
leakage analysis) is an **agent-only scratch reference** used to ground understanding of the
maths. It is **not** linked, compiled, or shipped, and will be deleted after the work is
done. The page presents a heavily simplified slice of its ideas.

### Example problem (shared across all figures)

- Domain: depth `z ∈ [0, 1]` (normalized).
- True model: 2 Gaussian bumps + a gentle ramp.
- Forward (SOLA): `dᵢ = ∫₀¹ Kᵢ(z) m(z) dz`, `N_d = 6` smooth Gaussian kernels at
  staggered depths.
- Noise: Gaussian, ≈2–3% of data RMS.

### Math beats to get right (review notes)

- Naive least-norm `α = Gᵀ(GGᵀ)⁻¹d` minimizes `‖α‖₂` (coefficients), not
  `‖m‖_{L²} = √(αᵀMα)` (the function).
- Geometry-correct least-norm: `α = M⁻¹Gᵀ(G M⁻¹ Gᵀ)⁻¹d`; adjoint `G* = M⁻¹Gᵀ`.
- Boxcars: `M = h·I` ⇒ naive == correct point estimate (the trap). Hat functions ⇒
  `M` non-diagonal ⇒ visible divergence.
- Convergence shown via **uncertainty** under refinement (σ²I diverges; proper covariance
  converges), not just the MAP.

### Figure inventory (generated in `intervalinf`)

- **F1** setup: true model + kernels + noisy data.
- **F2** naive boxcar recovery — "looks fine."
- **F3** naive vs geometry-correct on a **hat** basis — "the bug appears."
- **F4** N-refinement sweep — naive variance diverges vs correct converges (hero,
  interactive Plotly).
- **F5** BC prior samples (Neumann vs Dirichlet–Neumann).
- **F6** Discretize/Update/Push commute diagram as SVG (optional).

### Popup inventory ("?" theme)

- **Tier A (main readers):** equivalence classes; inner product = geometry;
  adjoint ≠ transpose; mass/Gram matrix.
- **Tier B (curious):** Hilbert vs Banach; trace-class / why σ²I fails; boundary
  conditions; quadrature accuracy; analysis/synthesis operators.
